# Automatic Fire / Cinematic Mechanism MG

24 seconds (720 frames at 30 fps). Original React / Three.js geometry in the browser, not an MP4, image sequence or video texture.

One abstract 3D model and one short caption at a time. A camera travels from exterior to cutaway, past the moving bolt and spring, down to the supply of cartridges, then back to the entire mechanism. Four visually slow cycles repeat while the trigger stays held. A fully automatic firing-control concept allows the cycle to fire again without another trigger press; unlike a semiautomatic mechanism, which requires release and another press.

The shapes, timings, dimensions and cycle choreography are illustrative, intentionally not an engineering or firearm modification reference. The visual does not model a real weapon's locking system, feed geometry or fire-control components.
