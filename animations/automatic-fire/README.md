# Why automatic fire repeats — cinematic 3D cutaway

Public route: https://siliconyc.github.io/MyMG/#/automatic-fire

The second version **replaces**, rather than decorates, the earlier rectangular sketch. React/Remotion/Three.js renders all 720 frames live; no MP4, pictures, video textures or frame sequences.

A one-piece original sculpture uses custom beveled and curved profiles for the stock, receiver, handguard, barrel, grip and magazine. A physical receiver-side plate lifts out toward the lens, exposing one turquoise reciprocating bolt, a helical spring and warm brass cartridge cues. Matte gunmetal on a warm neutral studio background replaces noisy technical overlays. Captions are short and transient.

The camera moves deliberately: wide three-quarter hero view, orbiting dolly into the opening, lateral tracking along the bolt, changed oblique views during repeated movement, then a graceful pull-back and sideplate closure. Camera pose, zoom, component motion and subtitles are pure frame-driven functions.

The four cycles happen without a new trigger press: the visible held-trigger cue contrasts automatic and semiautomatic repetition. **No functional fire-control, locking, calibre or conversion dimensions are represented. The animation is instructional art, not a fabrication reference.**

Validation: root pnpm check, pnpm build:portal, Chromium qa-automatic-fire.mjs with 12 screenshot pixel samples and actual WebGL context plus mobile/seeking checks.
